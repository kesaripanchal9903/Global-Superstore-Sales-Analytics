# Business Insights & Recommendations

This folder documents the key business findings identified through SQL analysis and the Looker Studio dashboard.

## Key Analysis Areas

1. Overall Business Performance
2. Year-wise Business Performance
3. Regional Business Performance
4. Category-wise Business Performance
5. Sub-Category Profitability
6. Impact of Discounts on Profitability
7. Customer Segment Performance
8. Customer Profitability Analysis
9. Shipping Mode Performance
10. Monthly Sales Trends

Each insight includes data-driven findings and actionable business recommendations.

## Objective

To identify sales trends, profitability drivers, underperforming areas, customer behaviour, and operational improvement opportunities.
