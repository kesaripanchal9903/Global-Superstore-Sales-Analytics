# Looker Studio Dashboard

This folder contains documentation for the interactive Sales & Business Performance Analytics dashboard created using Google Looker Studio.

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 – Executive Sales Overview
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Category-wise Sales and Profit
- Regional Sales and Profit
- Sub-Category Sales and Profit
- Country-wise Performance

### Page 2 – Customer & Product Analysis
- Top 10 Customers by Sales
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Customer Segment Sales and Profit
- Top 10 Loss-Making Products

### Page 3 – Shipping & Operational Analysis
- Shipping Mode Sales and Profit
- Order Priority Distribution
- Average Shipping Duration
- Shipping Cost by Shipping Mode

## Interactive Filters
- Date Range
- Region
- Category
- Segment
- Country
- Shipping Mode
- Order Priority
