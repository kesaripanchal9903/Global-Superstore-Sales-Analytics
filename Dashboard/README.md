# Global Superstore – Sales & Business Performance Dashboard

## Dashboard Overview

This interactive dashboard presents an analysis of Global Superstore sales, profitability, customer performance, product performance, and shipping operations.

**Dashboard Tool:** Google Looker Studio

**Live Dashboard:** [View Interactive Dashboard](https://datastudio.google.com/s/tOHBnfrkLFI)

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 – Executive Sales Overview

Provides an overview of overall business performance through:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Unique Customers
- Total Quantity Sold
- Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Category and Sub-category Performance
- Regional and Country Performance

**Filters:** Date Range, Region, Category, State, Country, City

### Page 2 – Customer & Product Analysis

Focuses on customer contribution and product profitability through:
- Top 10 Customers by Sales
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Sales and Profit by Customer Segment
- Top 10 Loss-making Products

**Filters:** Date Range, Segment, Category,  State, Country, City

### Page 3 – Shipping & Operational Analysis

Evaluates shipping performance and operational metrics through:
- Sales and Profit by Shipping Mode
- Order Priority Distribution
- Average Shipping Duration
- Shipping Cost by Shipping Mode

**Filters:** Date Range, Ship Mode, Order Priority, Category

## Interactive Filters
- Date Range
- Region
- Category
- Segment
- Country
- Shipping Mode
- Order Priority

## Technology Used

- Google Looker Studio
- Global Superstore Dataset
